    ✦ IN CASE OF NEED ✦

    Mark the Door

    Write “O Stree, Kal Aana” upon your threshold. She shall turn away till the morrow. A Kapidhwaj saffron banner strengthens the mark.

    Keep to Lit Lanes

    Avoid Katti Ghati and the stepwell after dusk. Travel in company; never answer a name called from behind.

    Emergency · 112

    For genuine danger, dial 112, India’s national emergency number.

    ✦ THE ALLIANCE ✦

    The Kapidhwaj Wardens

    Long before the Gazette was printed, a guild of banner-bearers kept watch over Chanderi’s gates. Their saffron kapidhwaj pennant is hung where the walls are thin, and tradition holds that Stree will not cross beneath it. The Wardens still supply the town with ward-cloth, lantern oil, and the knowledge of which doors to mark.

    Raise their banner to see wards bloom over every haunt on the chart, and watch tonight’s peril fall.
